Step 1 of 4

The staff is a picture of the string.

That thin line on the left is the 1st string of a guitar — the skinny one. Press it down further up the neck and the pitch goes up. Everybody already knows that part.

Here's the claim this page is going to make: the five lines of the staff are that same string, stood on end. A note sitting higher on the staff is a note further up the string. The staff isn't a code you memorize — it's a map, and up is up.

Two positions are open right now. Move the finger and watch what the notehead does.
